# EDD-102 User-Centered Interaction Foundation Review

## Review Objective
Review EDD-102 as a Product Owner against the EstateFlow Delivery Framework and confirm whether the document is suitable for governance approval as a product-definition artifact only.

## Review Summary
The EDD contains the required product-definition sections and remains appropriately scoped to business meaning and governance boundaries. The document clearly preserves the separation between Product definition and future Engineering implementation.

## Required Sections Assessment
The EDD contains the following required sections:
- Purpose
- Business Need
- User Problem
- Target Users
- Capability Scope
- Explicit Exclusions
- Business Rules
- Domain Impact
- Architectural Constraints
- Acceptance Criteria
- Verification Approach
- Risks and Assumptions
- Dependencies

## Assessment Against Governance Criteria
### Alignment with the approved Phase Two Charter
The EDD is aligned with the Phase Two direction of progressing from EDD-101 toward a broader user-centered capability foundation without exceeding the approved governance boundaries.

### Alignment with the EstateFlow Platform Vision
The EDD is aligned with the platform vision of preserving a clear and governed product foundation for future capability growth.

### Preservation of the EstateFlow v1.0 architectural boundaries
The EDD preserves the v1.0 architectural boundaries by avoiding implementation design, technical design, runtime behavior, and baseline change.

### Preservation of EDD-101 capability boundaries
The EDD preserves the EDD-101 capability boundaries by building on the identity foundation concept without expanding into implementation or security scope.

### Scope clarity
The scope is clear and bounded to product meaning, governance, and future-product planning.

### Product value
The EDD provides clear product value by defining a user-centered interaction foundation concept that can guide future capability planning.

### Traceability
The EDD is traceable to the approved Phase Two direction and to the EDD-101 identity foundation concept.

### Governance compliance
The EDD is governance compliant because it remains a product-definition artifact only and does not authorize implementation, engineering design, code changes, release activity, or baseline modification.

## Recommendation
Approved
