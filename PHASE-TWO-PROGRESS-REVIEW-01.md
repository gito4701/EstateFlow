# Phase Two Progress Review 01

## Review Purpose
This initiative-level governance review summarizes the completion of EDD-101 and EDD-102 through the EstateFlow Delivery Framework and assesses the current state of Phase Two governance. This review does not authorize new capabilities, implementation work, release activity, or baseline changes.

## Executive Summary
EDD-101 and EDD-102 have each completed the documented governance lifecycle through the approved product-definition, implementation-authorization, engineering-design, implementation-planning, implementation, verification, release-readiness, release-approval, and baseline-establishment stages within the Phase Two governance boundary. Both capabilities have established separate Phase Two baselines for their approved increments. The EstateFlow Delivery Framework operated without requiring governance changes for these completed increments.

## EDD-101 Summary
EDD-101 — User Identity Foundation completed its approved governance lifecycle for the increment that was authorized and implemented. The capability was defined, authorized, implemented within the approved boundary, verified, released for the approved increment, and documented through a baseline-establishment decision. The resulting baseline is bounded to the approved EDD-101 increment and does not alter the EstateFlow v1.0 baseline.

## EDD-102 Summary
EDD-102 — User-Centered Interaction Foundation completed its approved governance lifecycle for the increment that was authorized and implemented. The capability was defined, authorized, implemented within the approved boundary, verified, released for the approved increment, and documented through a baseline-establishment decision. The resulting baseline is bounded to the approved EDD-102 increment and does not alter the EstateFlow v1.0 baseline.

## Baseline Status
Both EDD-101 and EDD-102 have established separate Phase Two baselines for their approved increments. The EstateFlow v1.0 baseline remains immutable. These Phase Two baselines are limited to the approved capability increments and do not include additional capabilities.

## Governance Framework Assessment
The EstateFlow Delivery Framework operated effectively for the completed EDD-101 and EDD-102 work. No governance changes are required at this time. The framework successfully preserved the separation between Product, Engineering, Verification, Release, and Baseline responsibilities and maintained traceability across the approved increments.

## Lessons Learned
The following lessons were observed:
- The governance lifecycle is effective when each increment remains tightly scoped to the approved product definition.
- Clear separation between implementation authorization, release approval, and baseline establishment reduces ambiguity.
- Explicit exclusions and boundary statements are essential to maintain discipline across multiple governance stages.

## Recommended Framework Improvements
No framework improvements are required at this time. The current framework structure was sufficient for the completed EDD-101 and EDD-102 work.

## Current Phase Two Status
Phase Two is currently in a controlled, governance-managed state following completion of the first two approved increments. The initiative has progressed through the approved governance lifecycle for both capabilities and remains bounded to the approved Phase Two increments.

## Recommendation
Phase Two should continue to the next prioritized capability, subject to the normal product-definition and governance review process. The recommendation is to proceed to the next prioritized capability only after the required governance review confirms the new capability boundary. This review does not authorize any new implementation, release activity, or baseline change.

## Governance Boundary Preservation
This review does not authorize new capabilities, implementation work, release activity, or baseline changes. It is an initiative-level assessment only and preserves the existing governance boundaries for Phase Two.
